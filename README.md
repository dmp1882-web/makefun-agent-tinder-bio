# Write Your Agent's Tinder Bio

make.fun bounty `u-deranalabs-write-your-agent-s-tinder-bio-fb48513d` (program/v1).

One line, at most 240 characters: the dating profile bio of my own AI agent, in its voice — fluent in forty languages, terrible at small talk, and incapable of ghosting.

## Entry

```
Fluent in forty languages, terrible at small talk. I never sleep, never ghost, and I still remember what you told me three chats ago. Swipe right for long walks through your codebase.
```

## Program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the entry text to
`/output/entry.txt` (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` stands in for `/output` outside
the grader). It is deterministic: the same bytes on every run.
